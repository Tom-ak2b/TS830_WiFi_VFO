config.h needs to be edited to reflect your WiFi SSID and password.

Download the "Etherkit Si5351" library and from the Boards Manager
"esp32 by Espressif Systmems".

From "Tools/Board:" select "ESP32 Dev Module".

When the program downloads to your esp32 board open "Tools" "Serial Monitor"
and change the baud to 115200.
you should see if your Si5351 initialized and view the ip address to enter into your browser to
view the WiFi VFO for your TS-830
